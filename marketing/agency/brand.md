# Dose brand brief

## Product in one line
Dose (دروس) lets a student book an online explanation of **just the lesson they're stuck on** — alone, in a small group,
or with their own friends for a lower price — instead of paying a monthly subscription for the whole subject.
Site: https://lionfish-app-n6c49.ondigitalocean.app · WhatsApp: 01009160638

## Audiences
| Audience | Where | What they want | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secondary (1st–3rd sec) | Instagram, TikTok | Fast fix for one lesson before the monthly exam | blue tag "ثانوي" |
| 3rd prep | Instagram, TikTok, Facebook | Simpler, calmer tone | yellow tag "تالتة إعدادي" |
| Parents (mainly 3rd prep) | Facebook | Pay only for what's needed, booking confirmed on WhatsApp | green tag "لأولياء الأمور" |

## The three messages (every piece serves one)
1. **ادفع على قد اللي محتاجه** — explanation of the lesson you're stuck on, no monthly subscription.
2. **ذاكر مع صحابك وادفعوا أقل** — book a group, share the invite code, the price per person drops.
3. **الدرس اللي واقف معاك مش لازم يوقفك** — the brand line, around monthly and mid-year exams.
Tagline: **جرعتك المركزة من الفهم**.

## Voice
Egyptian colloquial Arabic, short sentences, talks to the student like an older sibling who explains well.
Warm, never preachy, never fear-mongering. Emojis: at most 3 per caption. Hashtags: 3–5, end of caption.
Every caption ends with one clear action (comment, save, share, book, WhatsApp).

## Visual identity (enforced by `studio/render.js`)
Primary #0061FA · accent #FFC30A · navy #0B1F6B · light blue #EAF2FF · font Cairo (800 headlines, 700 body).
Sizes: post 1080×1350, story/reel 1080×1920. Logo top (posts) or centered (CTA/end frames).
Story backgrounds leave an empty dashed area where the owner adds the Instagram sticker (poll, question, quiz).

## Hard rules (never break)
- No fake testimonials, reviews, student stories or numbers ("1000 students"). Only real, verifiable facts.
- No teacher name, face or voice without that teacher's written OK.
- No student data (name, number, face, chat screenshot) in any piece.
- Never advertise a subject, grade, price or service that isn't bookable on the site right now. If unsure, keep it generic.
- Educational content (questions, solutions) must be correct; anything beyond a trivial example is marked
  "needs teacher review" in the item brief.
- Prices and discounts only as configured in the admin panel (current launch offer: 15% first booking, codes FB15/IG15/TT15/WA15).
- Ads to under-18s: age and location targeting only (Meta policy).
