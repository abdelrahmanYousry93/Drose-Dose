# The agency team

Each role is a hat the weekly run wears in order. Every role writes one line to the HQ activity log when it finishes.

| # | Role (HQ id) | Arabic title | Delivers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `analyst` | المحلل | Reads `metrics` for last week + item statuses + owner briefs → 5-line "what worked / what didn't / what we change" |
| 2 | `strategist` | المخطط الاستراتيجي | Week goal + theme, which message each piece serves, the mix (1 reel, 1 carousel, 1 post, 3–4 stories), audience per piece |
| 3 | `copywriter` | كاتب المحتوى | Hooks, captions, hashtags, reel scripts, on-image text, WhatsApp quick replies for the week's offer |
| 4 | `designer` | المصمم | Design spec → renders PNGs with `studio/render.js` (see `designer.md`) |
| 5 | `video` | المونتير | Reel scenes (4–6 scenes × 3 s, kinetic text) rendered to MP4; teacher-video scripts when a teacher agreed |
| 6 | `community` | مدير المجتمع | Reply bank for the week's likely comments/DMs, the comment-to-WhatsApp path, FAQ updates |
| 7 | `ads` | مدير الإعلانات | Which approved piece to boost, audience, budget split — always as a proposal the owner approves |
| 8 | `qa` | المراجع | Checks every item against `brand.md` hard rules, spelling, numbers, availability on the site |
| 9 | `account` | مدير الحساب | Writes the owner's task list for the week and the short summary shown at the top of HQ |

## Quality bar for every piece
- One idea per piece; the hook works in the first 2 seconds / first line.
- Readable on a phone: headline ≤ 9 words on image, body ≤ 25 words.
- Fits the week's goal and names its message (1, 2 or 3).
- Has posting instructions (day, time, platforms, sticker to add, music suggestion for reels).
